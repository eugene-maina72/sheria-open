# SheriaOpen governance

## Purpose

SheriaOpen is governed to protect four things: legal accuracy, public trust, political neutrality and sustainable open-source development.

## Roles

### Maintainer

Maintainers manage repository access, releases, security processes, community standards and project direction. Maintainers may merge ordinary changes after review.

### Core contributor

Core contributors have a sustained record of useful contributions and may review code, documentation and operational changes within their areas of competence.

### Domain reviewer

Domain reviewers are trusted legal researchers, advocates or subject specialists approved to review legal explanations, source status, translations and high-risk content. A domain-review role is not automatically a maintainer role.

### Contributor

Any person who submits code, documentation, evaluation material, research, translations, design work or issue reports in accordance with project policies.

## Initial governance

During the founding phase, Eugene Maina acts as the initial maintainer. Additional maintainers and domain reviewers should be appointed as the project demonstrates sustained contribution and public use.

## Decision-making

Routine decisions are made through reviewed issues and pull requests.

Substantial decisions should use a public request-for-comments process. Examples include:

- changing the software licence;
- changing the authoritative-source hierarchy;
- replacing the main inference or embedding stack;
- collecting new categories of personal data;
- introducing paid features into the public instance;
- creating political or electoral features;
- changing the legal-status taxonomy; or
- entering a partnership that may affect perceived independence.

The maintainer seeks rough consensus. When consensus cannot be reached, the maintainer records the decision, evidence considered, objections and review date.

## Review requirements

- Ordinary code: one maintainer or authorised core-contributor approval.
- Security-sensitive code: one maintainer plus security review.
- Legal explanations and status mappings: domain-review approval before public release.
- Source-policy changes: maintainer and domain-review approval.
- Privacy-policy changes: maintainer approval and documented impact assessment.
- Licence changes: public RFC and explicit maintainer decision.

## Political neutrality

SheriaOpen must not endorse candidates, parties or electoral outcomes. Contributors may hold political views, but project outputs and official channels should remain focused on verifiable law, legislative process and civic education.

Sponsors and commercial customers may not purchase influence over legal explanations, evaluation results, source selection or corrections.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers should disclose relationships that could reasonably affect their judgement, including employment, representation, funding, political office, campaigning or direct involvement in a dispute. Disclosure does not always require recusal, but material conflicts should be handled by an uninvolved reviewer.

## Corrections

Substantive legal corrections should be documented in release notes or a public corrections log. Quietly editing a serious error without an audit trail is discouraged.

## Appointment and removal

A contributor may be appointed to a trusted role based on sustained quality, reliability, respectful conduct and relevant competence. Trusted access may be suspended or removed for security risk, misconduct, persistent neglect, undisclosed conflicts or repeated disregard of source and review rules.

## Project assets

Administrative access, domains, donation accounts, social accounts and deployment credentials should not depend permanently on a single person. As the project grows, at least two trusted maintainers should have recoverable access with documented controls.

## Amendments

Governance changes should be proposed publicly and should include the reason, expected impact and transition plan.
