# SheriaOpen Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SheriaOpen is committed to a respectful, inclusive and evidence-led community. Participation should be safe for developers, legal professionals, students, civic educators, translators and members of the public regardless of background, identity, experience or political opinion.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas or work;
- assume good faith while still requiring evidence;
- welcome questions from people with different technical or legal experience;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid partisan campaigning in project spaces;
- distinguish legal sources from personal or political opinions;
- accept correction when evidence changes; and
- help maintain an environment in which contributors can disagree without hostility.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation or discriminatory language;
- publishing another person's private information;
- sexualised attention or imagery;
- deliberate misinformation or fabricated legal citations;
- impersonating a public institution, advocate or project maintainer;
- political campaigning, candidate promotion or voter manipulation through project channels;
- pressuring reviewers to approve unsupported legal content;
- retaliating against people who report errors or misconduct; or
- disrupting project spaces after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community calls, project events, social channels managed by the project and private communications concerning project work.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Before public launch, replace this sentence with a monitored contact address or a documented private reporting channel. Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Reports should include the relevant links, dates, participants and a concise description. Maintainers will protect confidentiality as far as reasonably possible and will disclose conflicts before handling a report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may issue a private warning, remove content, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access or permanently remove a participant. Decisions will consider severity, pattern, intent, harm and willingness to correct behaviour.

No person should be the sole decision-maker in a serious case in which they are directly involved. Appeals should be reviewed by an uninvolved maintainer or trusted external adviser.

## Good-faith legal disagreement

Reasoned disagreement over legal interpretation is not misconduct. Contributors should cite sources, identify uncertainty and avoid presenting contested interpretations as settled fact.
